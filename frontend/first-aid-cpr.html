CARDIO-PULMONARY RESUSCITATION (CPR)

Push Hard & Fast in the Center of the Chest

Maintain 2 inches (5 cm) compression depth. Match hand pushes exactly with the sound pulse and flashing circle.

CRITICAL EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS

UNRESPONSIVE / NO PULSE

1. Immediate Chest Compressions

Do not check breathing for more than 10 seconds. Place interlocked palms on breastbone center. Push continuously 100-120/min until ambulance arrives.

SEVERE HAEMORRHAGE

2. Arterial Bleed Control

Apply heavy direct pressure with clean cloth. If arterial limb spurting does not cease within 60s, apply improvised tourniquet 2-3 inches above wound.

FOREIGN BODY CHOKING

3. Heimlich Abdominal Thrusts

Stand behind victim, wrap arms around waist. Place fist thumb-side just above navel. Deliver quick inward & upward thrusts until obstruction dislodges.
